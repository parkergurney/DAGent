# batch01 candidate tasks — sqlite-utils

Mined from `github.com/simonw/sqlite-utils` open issues (real material), plus 2 self-authored from reading `db.py`.
The dependency chain is real, not just sequenced: #763 builds the CREATE-TABLE introspection primitive, #762 is the first consumer (check constraints/comments), #602 extends the same preserved-schema plumbing to AUTOINCREMENT — genuine 3-deep.
